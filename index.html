What Meridian can do

Four faculties, one steady hand.

Each is its own discipline, yet they share the same page. Pick one above, or let Meridian choose the right instrument for the question.

I.

Deep Research

Reads widely, cites carefully, and tells you where a claim is thin — the way a diligent research assistant would.

II.

Draft & Refine

Turns a rough thought into clean prose, then edits with a light hand until it sounds like you, only rested.

III.

Reason in Steps

Works a hard problem out in the open — assumptions first, then the chain — so you can check the course, not just the port.

IV.

Perfect Recall

Holds the whole thread in mind — your notes, your files, last week's tangent — and brings the right page back on cue.
